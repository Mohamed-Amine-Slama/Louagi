# Louagi — Voiceover script (Tunisian Derja), Sousse → Tunis

Total length is 25s. Each line belongs to one scene and starts at the time shown. Speak
calmly and with a smile, like you're explaining it to a friend. Leave a small pause
wherever you see "…".

## Lines that should land on screen moments
- Line 2: say **"Tounes"** at about 4.2s, when the finger taps Tunis in the list.
- Line 4: say **"w hak réservit!"** at about 12.7s, on the green check and the bell.
- Line 7: say **"Louagi"** at about 22.6s, when the logo lands.

## Recording tips
- Record in a quiet room. A phone voice memo is fine; hold it about 20 cm from your mouth.
- Do one continuous take while the video plays muted, or record each line separately
  (name the files `vo-1` … `vo-7`).
- Leave about 1 second of silence before you start talking.
